Rules used

  • Range = previous day high to low (Fib 100% to 0%), with days in GMT+2.
  • Buy stop at the previous high, sell stop at the previous low, exactly. SL and TP are both 10% of the range (1:1).
  • When one order fills, the other is cancelled. Orders that don't fill stay until price breaks out. No new orders are placed while an order is pending or a trade is open.
  • After a trade closes, new orders are placed at the next day's open using the day before it.
  • The spread in the data (about 2–2.4 pips) is included: buys fill on Ask and close on Bid. If price gapped through a level, the fill is at the open price.
  • Each M1 bar was checked. No bar touched both SL and TP, so every result is clear.
